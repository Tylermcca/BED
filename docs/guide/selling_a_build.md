# Check an exported build before selling

[Export](exporting.md) adds credits and release notes to the verified game folder. Keep the whole copied folder together. These files help you review a release; they do not approve a game for sale or prove that every asset may be sold.

After **File > Export build...**, **Build saved files** and **Copy build to folder...**, read:

- **CREDITS.txt**: the BRUTAL credit and each included asset record's author, declared origin, licence and notes. A missing field is named **MISSING**. No records supplied does not mean the game contains no assets.
- **NOTICE.txt** and **licenses/**: the BRUTAL notice and licence. Keep the game's own **THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt** or other licence list too, if its build supplies one. BED's added files are not a complete list of everything another game may use.
- **LOGO_BRUTAL_WHT.png** and **BRUTAL-RELEASE-CHECKLIST.txt**: the logo and the steps to review its display and credits inside the game. A logo sitting in the folder does not show it to a player. Check the game's opening and credits yourself against the included licence.
- **STEAM-AI-DISCLOSURE-DRAFT.txt**: a worksheet for the person submitting the game. Check content made with AI before release, anything generated while playing, and the checks needed for that live generation. Complete and verify the answers yourself before submitting them.

An asset's permission to be used with AI does not tell you whether AI made it. BED marks that history as unknown. Ask the creator and review code, writing, sound, store material and other content outside the included asset records too.

Before sharing the folder, run the copied game outside BED, check its opening and credits, and resolve missing or uncertain rights. BED neither submits to Steam nor uploads the game.
